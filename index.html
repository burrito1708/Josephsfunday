JOSEPH'S FUN DAY PT. 5

As we proceed beyond high school, comes with it is the end of the annual traditional collaborative slideshow, Joseph's Fun Day. Memories are presented as a cumulative showcase of memes and slander worked on by a multitude of people, but is it really the end? I believe as an attempt to engage members of a forever-lasting community to a similarly forever-lasting project, something with great value should not end so early on. With this, I present to you, Joseph's Fun Day Part 5.
